# A shared proportional work/rest bar, plus the one-line text, summarizes a workout wherever it needs recognizing

A reusable visual component — a horizontal bar of adjacent sub-rectangles, one per Exercise occurrence (accounting for Set/Exercise rounds), each colored with the same work/rest colors already used for the exercise-row background, and widthed proportionally to that occurrence's duration — represents a workout's shape at a glance. It's used in two places: in the editor, near the total-duration label, summarizing the live draft; and on each row of the workout history list, alongside the one-line text summary (ADR for history list summary), summarizing a saved entry without opening it. The alternative was text-only summaries in both places.

Picked the shared visual component because duration and work/rest balance are exactly what a human pattern-matches fastest as a shape rather than as numbers, and because building it once as a reusable element (driven by the same flattened/summed duration data ADR-0003 already computes for playback) means the editor and history list stay visually consistent for free rather than maintaining two different "what does this workout look like" representations.
