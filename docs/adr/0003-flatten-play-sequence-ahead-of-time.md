# Flatten the Set/Exercise tree into a linear play sequence before playback starts, rather than walking the tree live

`ExerciseTimer` drives playback today by incrementing a single index into a flat list. With Sets and Rounds, we expand the tree into an ordered, flat sequence of "plays" once (on `start()`/`reset()`) — each play is one Exercise occurrence plus which round-of-Set and round-of-Exercise it represents — and keep `ExerciseTimer`'s existing index-increment logic operating over that expanded list. The alternative was a live tree-walk: tracking nested counters (current Set, round-of-Set, Exercise-within-Set, round-of-Exercise) and advancing the innermost first, carrying over to outer counters when exhausted, recomputed on every step.

Picked flattening because it lets `timerEvent`/`playCurrentExercise`/`onCurrentExerciseFinished` and the total-progress/duration math stay simple sums-over-a-list, matching their current shape, instead of being rewritten around nested-counter arithmetic. The tradeoff is that the flattened sequence is fixed at the moment playback starts — edits to the workout structure mid-run aren't reflected — but that's not a capability the app has today either, so nothing is lost. Workout sequence lengths are small (dozens of plays at most), so the flattened list's memory cost is negligible.
